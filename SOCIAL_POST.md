## LinkedIn

Every trader has a "never again" list. Mine lives in my head, and my head isn't reliable at 2 AM when the chart is red.

For the Walrus × Tatum Total Recall hackathon I built Second Thought, a pre-flight check for money moves. You tell it what you're about to do and why (sell 40% of my SUI, after a dip, feeling fearful, at 2 AM). It reads the wallet from Sui mainnet through Tatum, recalls the closest past moves from Walrus Memory on mainnet, and answers GO, PAUSE or STOP, with every reason linked to the memories behind it.

It never moves money. It just makes sure your past self gets a vote. And it learns: patterns from your own record, lessons written back to memory, and stricter warnings if you override one and lose.

Try it: https://second-thought-bay.vercel.app
